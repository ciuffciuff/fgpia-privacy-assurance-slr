# Deviations from the protocol

Reported as recommended by Kitchenham & Charters (2007).

| Item | Planned | Executed |
|---|---|---|
| Databases | Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ACM DL | ACM DL removed on 30 Sep 2026, before screening |
| Search timing | Searches after protocol approval | Searches of 27 Sep 2026 run while the protocol was being finalized; confirmed as definitive on 6 Oct 2026 |
| Pre-classification | On the screened set | Computed on 9,506 records (with ACM) and not recomputed; ordering aid only |
| Extraction | Double independent extraction | One extractor (AI-assisted first pass) + one checker for every study; disagreements recorded and arbitrated by a third reviewer |
| Snowballing | Until saturation | One backward/forward round (OpenAlex), 29 seeds; 4 new studies not used as seeds; 4 seeds without OpenAlex match not chased manually |
| Registration | — | Protocol not registered in advance; published in this repository |
