# Use of generative AI

Claude (Anthropic, Opus model family) was used as a support tool in the following stages of the review:

| Stage | Use | Human control |
|---|---|---|
| Screening | Deduplication support and title-based prioritization of records (signal bands) | Double, independent and blind screening by two reviewers (kappa = 0.935) |
| Snowballing | OpenAlex script (`scripts/snowballing_openalex.R`) and analysis of its output; abstract pre-screening of the 17 candidates | Decisions confirmed by the reviewers |
| Extraction | First pass of data extraction and quality pre-scoring (QA1–QA5) with the standardized form | Verified by a second reviewer; divergences arbitrated by a third |
| Synthesis | Quantitative consolidation of the extraction; first proposal of the FGP-IA activity specification | Reviewed and approved by the authors |
| Writing | Drafting and translation of the manuscript and of this data package | Reviewed by the authors |

All inclusion decisions, extracted data and scores were verified by the reviewers, who take full responsibility for the content.
